## Rights-check result

Rights were checked against the rights notes shipped with the admitted source snapshots and the permission notices visible in those captured pages. Public visibility and attribution are not treated as permission.

- **O*NET profile and task-rating dictionary:** each captured page contains its own CC BY 4.0 notice ([license text](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). The profile notice identifies the occupation profile; the dictionary notice identifies the dictionary page. Those notices do not grant the O*NET marks, logos, linked or dynamic pages, third-party content, or unrelated site material. The guide uses the profile as a linked source and credits it, but reproduces no verbatim O*NET wording or visual asset. The task-rating dictionary is not used to assign a rating.
- **ESCO pages:** the captured pages and manifest do not establish a page-specific reuse license. A page's description as linked/open data is not itself treated as a license for its prose, taxonomy, or embedded materials. No ESCO prose, data, or taxonomy content is used in the export; only citation metadata is retained.
- **OPM page:** the admitted rights note says reuse terms were not independently checked. Government attribution and public access are not treated here as blanket permission for page-specific or third-party material. No OPM wording or page asset is reproduced; its URL is retained for provenance.
- **DACUM page:** the admitted rights note says reuse terms were not independently checked, and the captured page includes an Ohio State copyright notice. No DACUM wording or asset is reproduced. The statement that this project did not conduct a DACUM panel describes this project's process, not a reproduced source passage.
- **Seven employer postings:** the admitted records identify employer-authored material retrieved from public feeds, but expressly do not establish reuse permission. Public visibility, feed access, source links, and attribution do not grant publication rights. The public guide includes no posting text, job-specific wording, close paraphrase, role example, title finding, matched phrase, count, or demand result. Only source-citation metadata and digests are retained below.

No rights clearance is inferred for any source whose terms remain unverified. No source expression from those unresolved sources is included in the export.

## Omission inventory

The following original-run material is intentionally absent from the public export:

1. **All direct source excerpts and embedded source wording** in the original guide, evidence-transparency view, and machine release, including O*NET task/skill text and quoted OPM, ESCO, DACUM, and task-rating-dictionary passages. The O*NET license notice was checked, but no source wording was needed in this export; unresolved-source text was excluded rather than paraphrased.
2. **All seven employer-posting contents:** titles, duty/responsibility wording, job-specific access conditions, requirements, and other excerpts. No close paraphrase or employer-specific context summary is substituted.
3. **All posting-derived analytical outputs:** phrase-screen matches, counts, demand statements, sample-context examples, and title-variant or exact-title findings. The public practice sequence is not ranked from postings.
4. **The original task–competency graph, work-unit list, and evidence quote inventory.** These contain sourced wording and separately labeled analyst link inferences that are not needed to publish the retained practice sequence.
5. **Any ESCO-, OPM-, or DACUM-source prose, taxonomy, figures, images, logos, or other source assets.** Their citation records do not convey a content license.

The source register preserves only attribution, links, retrieval metadata, and content digests. Those metadata neither reproduce source passages nor assert that the underlying content is licensed.
