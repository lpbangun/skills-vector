Quick Facts: Retail Sales Workers 2025 Median Pay $35,560 per year $17.10 per hour Typical Entry-Level Education No formal educational credential Work Experience in a Related Occupation None On-the-job Training See How to Become One Number of Jobs, 2025 4,271,400 Job Outlook, 2025–35 0% (Little or no change) Employment Change, 2025–35 -5,700 What Retail Sales Workers Do Retail sales workers sell merchandise, spare and replacement parts, and equipment to customers. Work Environment Retail sales workers usually work in clean, well-lit stores. Most are employed full time. Schedules may vary, with evening and weekend work required in some jobs. How to Become a Retail Sales Worker Retail sales workers typically do not need formal education to enter the occupation. However, some employers prefer to hire candidates who have a high school diploma or the equivalent. Most retail sales workers learn on the job. Pay The median hourly wage for parts salespersons was $18.57 in May 2025. The median hourly wage for retail salespersons was $17.03 in May 2025. Job Outlook Overall employment of retail sales workers is projected to show little or no change from 2025 to 2035. Despite limited employment growth, about 550,600 openings for retail sales workers are projected each year, on average, over the decade. Most of those openings are expected to result from the need to replace workers who transfer to different occupations or exit the labor force, such as to retire.
