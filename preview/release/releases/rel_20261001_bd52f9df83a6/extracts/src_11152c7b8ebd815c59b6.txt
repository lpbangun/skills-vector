Quick Facts: Advertising, Promotions, and Marketing Managers 2025 Median Pay $165,780 per year $79.70 per hour Typical Entry-Level Education Bachelor's degree Work Experience in a Related Occupation See How to Become One On-the-job Training None Number of Jobs, 2025 454,800 Job Outlook, 2025–35 6% (Faster than average) Employment Change, 2025–35 27,900 What Advertising, Promotions, and Marketing Managers Do Advertising, promotions, and marketing managers plan programs to generate interest in products or services. Work Environment Advertising, promotions, and marketing managers typically work in an office setting. They may travel to meet with clients or media representatives. Most of these managers work full time, and some work more than 40 hours per week. How to Become an Advertising, Promotions, or Marketing Manager Advertising, promotions, and marketing managers typically need a bachelor’s degree. They also typically need work experience in a related occupation. Pay The median annual wage for advertising and promotions managers was $133,660 in May 2025. The median annual wage for marketing managers was $166,790 in May 2025. Job Outlook Overall employment of advertising, promotions, and marketing managers is projected to grow 6 percent from 2025 to 2035, faster than the average for all occupations. About 36,300 openings for advertising, promotions, and marketing managers are projected each year, on average, over the decade. Many of those openings are expected to result from the need to replace workers who transfer to different occupations or exit the labor force, such as to retire.
