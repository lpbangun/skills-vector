Quick Facts: Software Developers, Quality Assurance Analysts, and Testers 2025 Median Pay $134,040 per year $64.44 per hour Typical Entry-Level Education Bachelor's degree Work Experience in a Related Occupation None On-the-job Training None Number of Jobs, 2025 1,905,400 Job Outlook, 2025–35 10% (Much faster than average) Employment Change, 2025–35 185,400 What Software Developers, Quality Assurance Analysts, and Testers Do Software developers design computer applications or programs. Software quality assurance analysts and testers identify problems with applications or programs and report defects. Work Environment Many software developers, quality assurance analysts, and testers work in computer systems design and related services, in manufacturing, or for software publishers. They often work in offices and on teams with other software developers or quality assurance analysts and testers. How to Become a Software Developer, Quality Assurance Analyst, or Tester Software developers, quality assurance analysts, and testers typically need a bachelor’s degree in computer and information technology or a related field. Some employers prefer to hire developers who have a master’s degree. Pay The median annual wage for software developers was $135,980 in May 2025. The median annual wage for software quality assurance analysts and testers was $104,300 in May 2025. Job Outlook Overall employment of software developers, quality assurance analysts, and testers is projected to grow 10 percent from 2025 to 2035, much faster than the average for all occupations.
