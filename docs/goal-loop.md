# Lightweight project goal loop

## Goal

Build a verified local foundation that can produce an evidence-led U.S. startup skills reference for three pilot occupations (founding engineer, product manager, growth operator), with a Jobsss-local assessment contract, without claiming the 30-role launch gate.

## Adaptive loop

At each meaningful increment:

1. Re-check scope: does the change strengthen the occupational catalog, provenance, review/release path, or assessment contract?
2. Choose the highest-leverage bounded task that reduces a named risk or proves a product invariant.
3. Make the smallest coherent change, then verify with offline tests or inspectable evidence.
4. Record a short result below.

## Guardrails and stop conditions

- UI follows Evidence Atlas Control in `DESIGN.md`. This slice does not add screens.
- Approved geographic scope: U.S. startup roles. People Operations briefs remain a prior experiment, not the product default.
- Job advertisements are stated demand. Social posts are emerging practice only.
- Production research never silently falls back to synthetic or fixture evidence.
- Publication is static and atomic. No Sites deploy, paid DB, or hosted scheduler in this phase.
- Monthly inference cap is US$10 with reservations before live DeepInfra calls. Paid usage stays $0 until credentials and an explicit live interpreter are configured.
- Stop when the three-role offline slice is verified, the same blocker stalls twice, or a permission is required.
