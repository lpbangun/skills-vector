# Investigation observability surface

- **Mode and job:** Operate. The local owner reviews a role brief, understands how the investigation produced it, and makes the private approval decision.
- **Proof and content:** Persisted logical stages, actual invocations, structured findings, material claims, source snapshots, uncertainty, disagreement, failure state, and approval eligibility.
- **Direction:** Brief & approval remains the default. Claim-level trace links lead into deterministic evidence clusters; the fixed phase graph reveals worker detail without drawing every invocation as a node.
- **Memorable moment:** A claim opens directly into its evidence cluster, where ranking reasons, connected findings, public provenance, and expandable local worker payloads are available in sequence.
- **Constraints:** Preserve the incumbent dashboard identity, remain deterministic and offline, keep partial drafts visible, block approval when required claims fail validation, paginate hundreds of sources, and add no model/API configuration.
