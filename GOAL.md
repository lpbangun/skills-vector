# Skills Vector Goal

Deliver the runnable, private local product in `SPEC.md`; contracts, plans, mockups, and unexecuted graphs are not completion.

## Adaptive execution loop

Repeatedly select the highest-leverage missing capability, make one coherent MVP-scoped change, and verify it. After meaningful increments, run a realistic role investigation. For every failure: identify the cause, make a focused fix, add regression coverage, and restart the passing streak.

The quality bar is three consecutive passing cases in this order: HR Coordinator, Recruiter, Learning & Development Specialist. Completion also requires concrete evidence for every `SPEC.md` done-when criterion. Stop early only for a materially required owner decision or two genuine no-progress attempts.

## Progress evidence

Progress is demonstrated by executable graph state, persisted database records, working HTTP/UI actions, automated test output, and repeatable CLI commands. Future prompt/policy/router improvement is deferred; any later promotion requires an experiment log, working cases, untouched holdouts, and regression-free evidence.

## Approval boundary

Investigations may deterministically create private drafts. Only the owner-provided reviewer action may approve one. Approval remains private and never publishes or triggers an external side effect.
